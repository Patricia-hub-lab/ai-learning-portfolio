# Embeddings

## What is an embedding?

An embedding is a vector representation of a token in a high-dimensional numerical space. Each token is converted into a numerical vector called an embedding. This mathematical representation captures characteristics and relationships learned by the model during training.

## What is the purpose of an embedding?

An embedding represents each token as a numerical vector, allowing the model to perform mathematical operations on language and capture relationships between tokens.

## Why are "cat" and "dog" likely to have similar embeddings?

"Cat" and "dog" tend to have similar embeddings because they appear in similar contexts and have related meanings. During training, the model learns these relationships from patterns in the text.

This does not mean that "cat" and "dog" are the same. It means that they tend to occupy nearby regions in the vector space because they have related semantic characteristics and contexts.

## What I learned

I learned that embeddings transform tokens into numerical vectors that allow models to represent and process relationships between words mathematically. Tokens with similar meanings or uses tend to be located closer together in the vector space.
