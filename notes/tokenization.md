# Tokenization

## What is tokenization?
Tokenization is the process of splitting text into smaller units called tokens, allowing the model to process language more efficiently. Tokens can represent complete words, parts of words, or punctuation. By reusing common parts of words, tokenization allows models to work with a more manageable and efficient vocabulary.
## Example

Consider the sentence:

> "O gato preto dormiu tranquilamente em cima do sofá azul."

A simplified tokenization could look like this:

"O"
" gato"
" preto"
" dormiu"
" tranquil"
"amente"
" em"
" cima"
" do"
" sofá"
" azul"
"."
This example shows that tokens do not always correspond to complete words. A word can be represented by a single token or divided into smaller parts. Punctuation can also be represented as a separate token.

## What I learned

I learned that tokenization allows models to reuse common parts of words. These parts can be shared across many different words, making it possible to represent a large variety of words using a more manageable vocabulary.
